1. Parties and scope
Example Studio provides monthly website maintenance to Demo Client.
The initial term runs from 1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026.

2. Payment
The client pays INR 5,000 per month, due on the fifth day of each month.
The provider may increase the monthly fee at any time without prior notice.

written notice at least sixty days before the end of the initial term.
